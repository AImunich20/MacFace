## MacFace 2.0.0

Faster, smoother face unlock, plus fixes for bugs reported against glance.

### Faster
- **The scan analyses every camera frame:** about 30 per second, up from about 18 in 1.x (measured with `tools/run_pipeline_benchmark.sh`). Liveness gets more samples, so it confirms sooner and catches quick blinks.
- **Camera warm-up on wake:** when the lock screen wakes and a hover or space press would start the scan, the camera starts in advance (it takes ~0.4–0.7 s). You can turn this off in Settings → General → "Warm up camera on wake".
- **Instant retry:** after a scan that didn't unlock, the camera stays on for 4 seconds, so a retry starts right away.
- **Better camera format:** the camera now uses the highest resolution that still runs at 30 fps or more, preferring 60 fps when available. Before, it could pick a slow high-resolution format.

### Smoother
- **Camera off the main thread:** starting, configuring and switching the camera no longer run on the main thread, so they can't stall the notch animation. Camera frames also no longer pass through the main thread during an unlock.
- **Animations start without delay:** the success and failure animations are loaded ahead of time.

### Fixes
- **Recovers after sleep or display changes:** MacFace recovers by itself when the camera stops responding after a long sleep, a monitor switch or a camera disconnect. The camera restarts if no frame arrives for 1.5 s (glance #49).
- **"On space" works after a plain lock:** pressing space now starts the scan after a lock without sleep. Before, the system was sometimes slow to report the lock and the space key was never listened for (glance #54).
- **Passwords with repeated characters:** passwords with consecutive spaces or other repeated characters are now typed correctly on the lock screen (glance #66).
- **Wrong face doesn't end the scan early:** when a longer detection time is set, a non-matching face no longer ends the scan after 2 seconds. The grace period is now 60% of the detection time, and never less than 2 seconds (glance #48).
